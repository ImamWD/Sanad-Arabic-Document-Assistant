## Recitals

A. The Grantee has supported girls' basic education in Sahl Al-Qamar governorate since 2016. Its work builds on a 2015 study of girls' enrolment, which identified distance to school, early marriage, the cost of supplies and the lack of separate latrines as the main reasons girls leave school.

B. Since 2016 the Grantee has piloted school kits, mothers' councils and community awareness sessions in 12 schools, with encouraging early results.

C. The Trust wishes to support the Grantee in extending this approach to 30 public basic schools in the governorate.

The parties therefore agree as follows.

<!-- page 2 -->

## Clause 1. Purpose

The grant shall be used solely to implement project GEP-2019-02 (the "Project") as described in the approved proposal (Annex A). The Project aims to increase girls' enrolment and attendance in 30 public basic schools through four components:
- school kits
- teacher training in inclusive, gender-responsive teaching
- girl-friendly latrine blocks
- community mobilisation

## Clause 2. Duration

The Project shall run for 36 months, from 1 September 2019 to 31 August 2022. Any extension requires the Trust's prior written approval.

## Clause 3. Grant amount and payments

3.1 The Trust shall provide a grant of USD 900,000 (nine hundred thousand US dollars).

3.2 The grant shall be paid in four instalments:

| Instalment | Amount (USD) | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| First | 300,000 | Signature of this Agreement |
| Second | 250,000 | Approval of the first annual report |
| Third | 250,000 | Approval of the mid-term evaluation and second annual report |
| Final | 100,000 | Approval of the final report |

3.3 Payments shall be made to the Project account designated in Annex C, which is not reproduced in this copy.

## Clause 4. Expected results

The Grantee shall work towards the following results by the end of the Project. Baseline values will be confirmed from school records in September 2019.

| Indicator | Baseline | Target (August 2022) |
|---|---|---|
| Girls' enrolment rate in the 30 target schools | 61% | 75% |
| Girls receiving a full school kit | 0 | 3,600 |
| Teachers trained in inclusive teaching | 0 | 150 |
| Girl-friendly latrine blocks completed | 0 | 30 |
| Schools with an active mothers' council | 4 | 30 |
| Girls' attendance rate (share of school days) | 78% | 90% |

The Grantee shall report on every indicator in each annual report. Data shall be broken down by school and by grade where possible.

The Trust recognises that enrolment and attendance also depend on factors outside the Grantee's control, including security, the economy and education policy. Missing a target will not in itself be treated as a breach of this Agreement, provided the Grantee has reported promptly and taken reasonable corrective action.

## Clause 6. Reporting

6.1 The Grantee shall submit:
- a narrative and financial report every twelve months, within 45 days of the end of each Project year
- a final report within 90 days of the end of the Project

6.2 Financial reports shall compare actual spending with the budget in Clause 5. They shall be certified by the Grantee's finance director.

## Clause 7. Financial management and audit

The Grantee shall keep separate accounting records for the Project. The Project shall be covered by the Grantee's annual external audit, and the Trust may commission its own audit at its own cost.

## Clause 8. Safeguarding

The Grantee shall apply a written child safeguarding policy to all staff, volunteers and contractors working on the Project. It shall report any safeguarding concern to the Trust within 48 hours. No photographs of beneficiaries may be published without informed consent from the girl and a parent or guardian.

## Clause 9. Evaluation

9.1 An independent mid-term evaluation shall be carried out about 15 months after the start of the Project. Its findings shall inform any changes to the Project during its second half.

9.2 An independent final evaluation shall be carried out after the end of the Project, using the indicators in Clause 4.

9.3 Both evaluations shall be commissioned by the Grantee from evaluators approved by the Trust, and paid for from the monitoring and evaluation budget.

## Clause 10. Amendment and termination

This Agreement may be amended only in writing, signed by both parties. Either party may terminate it with 90 days' written notice. In the event of termination, the Grantee shall be reimbursed for commitments properly made before the date of notice.
